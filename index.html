Frequently Asked Questions

How far is the suite from Banff & Canmore?
We are strategically located on the West side of Calgary with instant access to Highway 1. You reach Canmore in ~50 minutes and Banff National Park in ~1 hour without navigating city traffic.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 04:00 PM onwards and Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Keyless self check-in codes are sent automatically prior to your arrival.
What is the payment & cancellation policy?
50% is due at reservation, and the remaining 50% is due 7 days prior to check-in. Prepayments are non-refundable.
